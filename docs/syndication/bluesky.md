# Bluesky

Inkstead can publish posts directly to Bluesky.

Required environment variables:

- `BLUESKY_IDENTIFIER`
- `BLUESKY_APP_PASSWORD`

`BLUESKY_IDENTIFIER` is usually your handle, such as `example.com` or `you.bsky.social`. Use an app password rather than your main account password.

Titled posts syndicate as the title plus canonical URL. Untitled notes syndicate as native social posts. Photo notes embed up to four images.
